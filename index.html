Meet the performer

Belle de Beauvoir

Belle de Beauvoir is an international cabaret and burlesque performer, singer, comedian and host, bringing glamour, wit and unapologetic showmanship to stages across the UK and internationally.

A fixture of the contemporary burlesque and cabaret scene, Belle has performed sold-out shows at the Edinburgh Festival Fringe and across Australia and the UK, captivating audiences with her unique blend of classic showgirl glamour, modern striptease, comedy and live performance.

Belle is a true triple-threat performer.
